OTR VIDEO SWITCHER - EASY INSTALL
=================================

1. Extract the entire ZIP.
2. Close OBS Studio.
3. Double-click INSTALL-OTR-VIDEO-SWITCHER.bat.
4. Approve the Windows administrator prompt.
5. Reopen OBS.
6. Under Sources, click + and choose OTR Video Switcher.
7. Open its Properties, select your Video 1, Video 2, and Video 3
   files, then use the Test / Play buttons to confirm them.

IMPORTANT:
Create the OTR Video Switcher source only once.
Use Add Existing to place the same source into every horizontal and
Aitum Vertical scene. This keeps the selected video, animation, and
whoosh synchronized across both canvases.

STREAM DECK / VSD DECK
======================

The plugin installs these keyboard controls automatically:

  Ctrl+Alt+1  Play Video 1
  Ctrl+Alt+2  Play Video 2
  Ctrl+Alt+3  Play Video 3
  Ctrl+Alt+0  Stop all videos

In the Stream Deck or VSD Deck software, drag a Keyboard Hotkey action
onto each key and record the matching shortcut. Keep OBS running while
using the deck. If the shortcuts were removed in OBS, open the OTR Video
Switcher Properties and click "Restore Ctrl+Alt+1 / 2 / 3 / 0 Shortcuts".

VIDEO AUDIO AND MONITORING
==========================

1. Start one of the videos so OTR Video Switcher is active in the Audio Mixer.
2. Open Advanced Audio Properties from the OBS Audio Mixer menu.
3. Find OTR Video Switcher and choose the Audio Monitoring mode you want:

   Monitor Off
     The audience hears it; you do not monitor it locally.

   Monitor Only (mute output)
     You hear it; it is not sent through that OBS mix.

   Monitor and Output
     Both you and the audience hear it.

4. Use the normal OTR Video Switcher mixer fader, mute button, and audio
   track checkboxes. The selected video's audio and whoosh2 share this
   one channel.
